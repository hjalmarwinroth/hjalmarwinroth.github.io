title: 不滿
author: Saltguy
date: '2026-02-13'

Satisfaction is not a permanent state. Rather quickly, one's great accomplishments become felt as immutable parts of oneself. The standard is raised, and one is forced to reach farther and farther up.

It's nature's fail-safe against stagnation. It brought humanity to travel from X to Australia in a rather rickety boat, with uncertain prospects of survival.

Nature does not optimize for our contentedness; this much is plain.
